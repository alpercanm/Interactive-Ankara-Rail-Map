          About this map

          Where the facts come from

          Lines, stations, lengths, dates and project status come only from EGO: the Raylı Sistem Hizmetleri page on ego.gov.tr (saved 8 November 2026) and EGO’s network video graphic.

          Routes and planned stations were traced from EGO’s graphic and aligned to the city’s road network. EGO’s M2, for example, lands on Eskişehir Yolu to within about 50 metres, and drawn line lengths come within a few percent of EGO’s figures. Stops on running lines sit at their approximate real-world positions along those routes. Use the map for orientation, not engineering detail.

          Your lines

          Lines you draw take the lowest free number from M7 up. Lengths are measured along the route as drawn, and new stations are named after the nearest neighbourhood until you rename them.

          Map data

          Roads, street names, neighbourhoods, parks, schools and hospitals © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), from the AnkaraHostpital, ankara_cflp and turkiye-district-gis projects on GitHub. Only main and secondary roads were available, so side streets aren’t drawn. District boundaries from Turkey’s General Directorate of Mapping (HGM), from the Ankara-district-polygons project on GitHub.
